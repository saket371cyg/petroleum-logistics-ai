# Dispatch Risk Playbook

- Before dispatch, check traffic, weather, route congestion, and loading-slot availability.
- If weather risk is high, re-plan the dispatch to a later slot and re-check driver availability.
- For a route with repeated delays, move the dispatch to a lower-risk corridor or identify an alternate tanker.
- Make sure ETA and risk are communicated to operations before the fleet leaves the depot.
- Use a dry-run plan when a decision is still pending human approval.
