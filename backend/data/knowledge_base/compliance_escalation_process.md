# Compliance Escalation Process

- Escalate any expired license, permit, or safety certification to operational compliance.
- A single expired compliance record is enough to block a dispatch recommendation.
- Log the risk level and the action taken, including whether the requirement was waived or deferred.
- Keep the final recommendation transparent about why a manual review is needed.
- For major compliance gaps, prefer an alternate route or supplier rather than a risky workaround.
