# Tanker Maintenance Guidelines

- Inspect tanker tires, brakes, and vehicle condition before long-haul dispatches.
- Schedule preventive servicing before the next due date if the maintenance risk score is high.
- Check oil, coolant, and fuel leaks before approving a dispatch.
- Twin-check license, permit, insurance, and age-related compliance before assigning a tanker to a route.
- If a vehicle shows repeated maintenance flags, plan an alternate tanker or shift to a later slot.
