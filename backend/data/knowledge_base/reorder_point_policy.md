# Reorder-Point Policy

- Reorder when projected demand and lead time suggest the stock will fall below the reorder point.
- Hold a minimum safety stock buffer equal to the target service level for that fuel.
- A critical stock condition should trigger an immediate order recommendation and an executive escalation.
- Validate that the recommended quantity does not exceed the physical tank capacity or approved budget.
- For diesel-heavy dealers, treat demand volatility as a higher-risk driver for early replenishment.
