# License and Permit Renewal Process

- Check all tanker and driver documents 30 to 60 days before expiry.
- Renew permits and insurance before the expiry date to avoid operational compliance alerts.
- Keep a log of renewal dates and document proof of submission.
- If a document is already expired or near expiry, escalate to compliance before approving a dispatch.
- Do not allow a dispatch to proceed on a non-compliant tanker or driver when an alternative exists.
