# Fuel Storage Safety SOP

- Keep diesel and petrol tanks within the approved operating band to avoid overflow risk.
- Check daily inventory and compare current stock with the safety stock and reorder point.
- Maintain a visible dispatch and receiving log for every fuel movement.
- If a tank is above 90% capacity or a risk alert is critical, escalate to depot management before dispatch.
- In India, ensure all storage and handling practices follow local fire-safety and environmental guidance.
